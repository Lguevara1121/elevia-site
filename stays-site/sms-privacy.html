SMS Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

This policy covers SMS consent collected by Elevia Enterprises LLC for its Elevia Stays service and Nexus assistant.

Information we collect and why

When you choose the optional SMS checkbox, we retain your name, mobile number, consent choice, submission time, consent wording version and the form source with your inquiry. We use this record to document your permission and support messages about your inquiry, appointments and requested service updates.

No marketing sharing

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Consent applies only to Elevia Enterprises LLC. Messaging and hosting service providers may process the information solely to deliver and support our services on our behalf, subject to appropriate restrictions.

Your choice

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. You may submit an inquiry without opting in. Reply STOP to stop texts, or HELP for assistance. We retain consent and opt-out records as needed to honor your choices and document compliance. Contact hello@eleviastays.com with privacy questions or requests.
